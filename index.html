Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is NX Downloader?

NX Downloader is a modern web application designed to resolve media streams and details from YouTube links directly inside your browser.

How do I download a video?

Simply paste a YouTube video link into the box above, click "Download", select your target format/quality, and trigger the download link.

Which platforms are currently supported?

Currently, YouTube links are fully supported. Additional platform integrations are planned for future releases.

Why couldn't my video be resolved?

This can occur if the link is invalid, private, age-restricted, region-blocked, or if the public endpoint experiences temporary network rate limits.

Can I use NX Downloader on mobile?

Yes, NX Downloader features a fully responsive Liquid Glass UI optimized for smartphones and tablets.
